# DESIGN.md --- Dr. Dnyaneshwar M. Mate Academic Portfolio

## 1. Project Overview

Build a premium, distinctive, modern academic portfolio website for
**Dr. Dnyaneshwar M. Mate**, Associate Professor of Mechanical
Engineering at JSPM's Rajarshi Shahu College of Engineering, Pune.

This is not a traditional CV website. It should feel like a **physical
engineering research journal transformed into an interactive digital
experience**.

Core concept:

> **Modern academic portfolio + engineering blueprint + paper-cut
> layers + tactile editorial design + ambient mechanical background
> video.**

The supplied reference screens are **information-layout references
only**. Do not copy their colors, typography, cards, spacing, dark
theme, or visual effects.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Creative Direction

Combine three visual worlds:

### Paper / Editorial

-   subtle paper grain
-   layered paper sheets
-   selective torn/cut edges
-   soft physical shadows
-   research-notebook details
-   editorial typography

### Engineering

-   blueprint line work
-   CAD-inspired geometry
-   technical annotations
-   measurement marks
-   subtle gears and mechanical silhouettes
-   thin engineering diagrams

### Modern Interaction

-   smooth scrolling
-   floating navigation
-   layered cards
-   hover elevation
-   parallax
-   scroll reveals
-   paper-cut transitions
-   subtle depth

The result should feel **human-designed, tactile, academic and
memorable**, not like an AI-generated SaaS dashboard.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Color System

The site must be predominantly **light**.

### Primary

-   Paper White: `#F8F7F3`
-   Warm White: `#FFFDF8`
-   Blueprint Blue: `#DCEBF0`
-   Steel Blue: `#607984`
-   Graphite: `#263238`
-   Warm Terracotta: `#C96A45`
-   Muted Brass: `#B49A67`

Primary hierarchy:

**Paper white → graphite → blueprint blue → terracotta accent**

Use terracotta sparingly for CTAs, active states, important numbers and
timeline markers.

Avoid dark navy, neon blue, purple gradients, cyberpunk colors, glowing
borders, and excessive gradients.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Paper Texture

Use a very subtle paper texture: - fine grain - soft fiber variation -
barely visible noise - off-white variation

Do not make it look like old scanned paper.

Engineering diagrams may sit underneath the paper layer at very low
opacity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Paper-Cut Visual Language

Use physical-looking layered paper selectively: - section title sheets -
research cards - paper strips - index tabs - bookmarks - pinned notes -
document sheets - folded corners

Layers should have slightly different whites, realistic separation and
soft natural shadows.

Do not turn every UI element into a paper card.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Paper-Cut Animation

Animations should feel physical and restrained.

Examples: - page-like transitions between major sections - paper layers
sliding upward during scroll - technical drawings revealing beneath
paper - headings appearing as if printed - cards lifting slightly on
hover - research documents opening - books subtly lifting/rotating -
timeline sheets entering sequentially

Avoid bouncing, spinning, neon effects or excessive 3D.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Background Video

Use the supplied engineering video as the ambient global background.

The video reference contains: - pale light-blue blueprint/grid
background - grey mechanical gears - white/light-grey gears - nuts and
bolts - mechanical components - slow engineering motion

The video is an **environment, not the content**.

Treatment: - low opacity - light overlay where required - slight blur
only if needed - mild desaturation only if needed - never turn it into a
dark background - never apply neon overlays

Recommended layer order:

1.  Engineering video
2.  Subtle light paper/noise overlay
3.  Engineering line-art / annotations
4.  Paper-cut content layers
5.  Text and UI

Use a light translucent paper surface behind dense text for readability.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Typography

Use a refined modern sans-serif with an editorial feel.

Use a technical/monospace font only for tiny labels or engineering
annotations.

Avoid sci-fi or futuristic fonts.

Typography should feel: - academic - premium - calm - authoritative -
highly readable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Navigation

Create a floating navigation bar that resembles a paper strip.

Use: - warm-white translucent surface - subtle paper texture - thin
graphite border - soft shadow - restrained blur

Navigation: - Home - Academics & Experience - Research & Publications -
Achievements - Contact

Active state: small terracotta tab/underline.

On scroll, compact the navigation slightly.

On mobile, use a compact floating menu.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 16. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

### Desktop

-   wide editorial compositions
-   layered paper
-   floating navigation
-   large portrait
-   ambient video
-   horizontal statistics
-   multi-column archives

### Tablet

-   reduce paper complexity
-   reduce columns
-   reduce typography
-   reduce animation intensity

### Mobile

-   single column
-   stacked paper layers
-   compact floating navigation
-   touch-friendly filters
-   readable typography
-   simplified paper animations

Do not merely shrink desktop.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. ACCESSIBILITY

Support: - strong contrast - keyboard navigation - visible focus
states - semantic headings - accessible buttons - reduced-motion mode

With reduced motion enabled, reduce parallax, paper movement, background
animation and scroll effects.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. PERFORMANCE

The background video must not block the site.

Use: - optimized video - poster fallback - lazy loading where
appropriate - muted autoplay - playsinline - mobile optimization -
reduced-motion fallback

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19. INTERACTION PRINCIPLES

Good: - slight paper lift - paper layer shift - tab slide - document
reveal - timeline movement - subtle image scale

Bad: - bouncing - spinning - neon glow - excessive 3D - constant
motion - distracting transitions

Animation should support the story, not become the story.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. FINAL DESIGN IDENTITY

The final website should be:

**LIGHT + TACTILE + EDITORIAL + ENGINEERING-INSPIRED + ACADEMIC +
PREMIUM + HUMAN + INTERACTIVE**

The key metaphor:

> **A professor's physical research notebook, engineering blueprint and
> academic archive brought to life as a modern interactive website.**

Do not make it look like: - a traditional faculty CV - a generic
portfolio template - an AI SaaS landing page - a dark futuristic
dashboard

Make it look like a **distinctive digital engineering research
journal**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
